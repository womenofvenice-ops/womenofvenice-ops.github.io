About the Artist

Claudio · Photographer & Storyteller

My name is Claudio. I am a photographer based in Venice, drawn to the city's light, its quiet corners, and the stories carried by the women who move through its streets.

Women of Venice began as a personal exploration — a way to meet the city through its people, one portrait at a time. Over time, it has become a living archive of encounters, a collection of faces and voices that together tell a story about Venice that you won't find in guidebooks.

I believe that every portrait is a collaboration — a moment of trust, a shared silence, a conversation. My job is not to capture a person, but to create a space where she can reveal herself.

When I am not photographing, I am walking the canals, drinking espresso, and listening to the city's endless whisper.

— Claudio
